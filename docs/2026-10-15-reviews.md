Two independent reviewers checked the first commit (`a2371bd`) and relay's `saveInstructions` change (relay `bb1ec3d`) against the user's requirements, before the skill was installed. Each worked in its own fresh clones and changed no tracked files.

- **omp, `openai-codex/gpt-6.1-sol` at high reasoning,** through the `use-omp` wrapper with memory off. About 9 minutes, $0.74. It backed its findings with model-free reproductions: relay's real `extractNote`, extracted grader predicates, and a broken-YAML copy.
- **Claude Opus, independent agent.** About 7 minutes. It verified relay's test against a reverted wiring, ran relay's `extractNote` on crafted input, and checked the user's real repositories against the preferences file.

Both reviewers found relay's `saveInstructions` wiring sound. Neither found the skill's structure out of line with the spec or the authoring guidance. Their findings were about behavior at the edges, eval rigor and docs. Every finding is listed below with what was done about it.
